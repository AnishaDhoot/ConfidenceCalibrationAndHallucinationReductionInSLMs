FFCS Academic Regulations, v4.0

Key regulations

  • Attendance: 75% minimum per course; below that you are debarred from the next CAT/FAT.
  • Course code: four letters, three digits, one letter (e.g. BCSE306L). The first digit shows the cognitive level.
  • Credits: one theory credit is 15 lecture hours; one lab credit is at least 30 hours.
  • Re-evaluation: a change of more than 5% sends the script to a different examiner; the best of the three marks counts.
  • Malpractice in a CAT: zero marks in that CAT for every course registered that semester. In the FAT: an N grade in the courses attended.
  • Audit courses: need a CGPA of at least 8.0 and show as a U grade; they do not count toward CGPA.

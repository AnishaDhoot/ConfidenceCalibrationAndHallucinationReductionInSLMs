Code of conduct highlights

  • Silence must be kept in the hostel from 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
  • Entering the campus or hostel gate without an ID card: Rs.100 the first time, Rs.1000 on repetition.
  • Electrical appliances in a hostel room: Rs.500 fine and confiscation.
  • Giving false information during a misconduct enquiry: Rs.3,000.

Student life

Clubs, chapters & fests

Student organisations are grouped into technical, social outreach, recreational (arts & culture) and literary clubs, alongside professional chapters and special teams.

Clubs

Technical, Social (Outreach), Recreational (Arts & Cultural) and Literary.

Chapters & teams

Professional chapters such as IEEE and ACM, plus special teams like Formula, Baja and Aero.

Fests

graVITas and Riviera are the big annual events, run jointly by many clubs and chapters.

From the academic regulations

Rules students ask about most

  • Minimum 75% attendance in each course to write the next CAT/FAT.
  • Students with CGPA ≥ 9.00 and no backlogs are exempt from the attendance minimum.
  • CAT: 90 minutes, 50 marks. FAT: 3 hours, 100 marks. Continuous assessment carries 60%, FAT 40%.
  • Absent from or debarred in the FAT means an N grade and re-registration.
